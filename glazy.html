2How to use it

Set up together, in a minute.

  1. Open Glazy on your phoneGo to glazy.electrosparkzz.in. Install it so it behaves like a normal app. On Android, tap Install (or ⋮ → Add to Home screen). On iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. On your home screen it shows up as Kitty Timer with a stopwatch icon.
  2. Tap the kitty 5 timesGlazy opens as a Kitty Timer. Tap the kitty five times quickly to create your couple space.
  3. Fill in both of youPartner 1 enters a name, a Glazy ID (like sam.k) and a 6-digit PIN, then hands the phone over. Partner 2 does the same. IDs are checked as you type, so you’ll know straight away if one is taken.

2How to use it, continued

  1. Pick whose phone it isGlazy creates your space and logs that phone in.
  2. Log in on the other phoneOpen Glazy, tap the kitty three times, then enter your ID and PIN.
  3. Every time after thatThree taps on the kitty, then your PIN. If Glazy sits in the background for a while, it goes back to looking like a timer.

Turn on notifications in the app to get messages and calls even when Glazy is closed. On iPhone this needs iOS 16.4 or newer, with Glazy added to the Home Screen.

3Privacy & encryption

End-to-end encrypted, for real.

Your messages are locked on your phone before they are sent, and only your partner’s phone can unlock them. Our servers store scrambled data they cannot read.

  • One key per couple. When you register, your phone creates a random 256-bit key just for your couple. Everything is encrypted with it using AES-256-GCM.
  • Locked with your PIN. Each of you gets a copy of that key, locked with a key made from your own PIN (PBKDF2-SHA-256, 600,000 rounds). The server only ever holds these locked copies.
  • Your PIN never leaves your phone. To log in, your phone sends a value derived from your PIN, not the PIN itself. The server mixes it with a secret only it knows before checking it.

3More on privacy

  • Guessing is stopped. After 5 wrong PINs, that ID is locked for 15 minutes.
  • Couples are sealed off. Database rules make sure each couple can only reach its own data, live rooms and files. Even a signed-in user can’t touch another couple’s space.
  • Calls are encrypted too. Calls use WebRTC (DTLS-SRTP) and go straight between your phones when possible. A relay server, when one is needed, only passes the scrambled audio and video along.

What is not

To make the app work, the server can see: your Glazy IDs and display names, when messages were sent and read, message types, reaction emojis, love taps, points, garden builds and avatar outfits. It never sees what you wrote, said or shared.

4Your data, your control

  • Unused spaces are deleted. If neither of you opens Glazy for 30 days, your space is permanently deleted: both accounts, every message and every file.
  • Logging out wipes Glazy’s data from that phone.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling. Glazy has no analytics and no advertising.

Please remember

  • Your PIN can’t be recovered. Because only your phone can unlock your key, nobody can reset a forgotten PIN. Not even us.
  • Keep your PIN to yourself, and avoid easy ones like birthdays.
  • Glazy is for people 18 and older, two people per space.

5Questions

Good to know.

Can Electrosparkzz read our chats?

No. Chats, photos and voice notes are encrypted on your phones with a key we never have. We only store scrambled data.

Can someone else join our space?

No. A space is created for exactly two people at once, and nobody can be added afterwards.

I got a new phone. What now?

Open Glazy on the new phone, tap the kitty three times and log in with your ID and PIN. Your history is still there.

I forgot my PIN.

A PIN can’t be reset, because it’s what unlocks your encrypted data. If it’s lost, you’ll need to create a new space together.

Does it cost anything?

Glazy is free during the early preview.

Why does it look like a timer?

So a glance at your screen doesn’t give your private space away. It’s a real, working timer, and the secret taps only matter if you know them.
